Solving Two-Step Equations

Example

\[3x + 5 = 20\]

Steps

  1. Subtract 5 from both sides.
    \[3x + 5 - 5 = 20 - 5\]
    \[3x = 15\]
  2. Divide both sides by 3.
    \[\frac{3x}{3} = \frac{15}{3}\]
    \[x = 5\]
  3. Check the answer.
    Substitute 5 for x:
    \[3(5) + 5 = 20\]
    \[15 + 5 = 20\]
The solution is \(\boxed{x = 5}\).
